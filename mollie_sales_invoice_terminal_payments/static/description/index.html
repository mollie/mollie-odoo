Direct Payments Terminal

Take in-person terminal payments for your sales orders and invoices.

Settle your open invoices and sales orders instantly with a physical terminal. This integration adds a direct payment button to your Odoo records, allowing you to trigger a transaction on your Mollie device with a single click. It eliminates the need for manual data entry and ensures every face-to-face payment is correctly linked to its document.

Accept payments via cards, QR codes and contactless payments.

How to configure

Step 1 Download the module and place the module in apps folder.

Step 2 Install this module in your Odoo instance.

Step 3 Open Sales > Configuration > Payment Providers and open mollie provider.

Step 4 Click on the Mollie Terminal tab. And then click on View terminals .

Step 5 Click on Sync terminal to sync terminals from your account.

DONE That's it you are ready. You will see the mollie terminal payment buttons on sale order and invoices records.
